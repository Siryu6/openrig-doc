## Black screen, nothing on SSH

Put the card in a computer and edit `cmdline.txt`: on the Pi, on the `SYSTEM` partition in use
(partition 2 on a new card); on a mini PC, `EFI/BOOT/grub.cfg`. Remove `quiet loglevel=3` and add
`console=tty1` to see the boot messages on the screen. Keep it all on one line.

## Wrong resolution, or no picture on a small screen

Some small panels send no or wrong display data (EDID), or are not ready when the Pi starts.
Add the mode at the end of `cmdline.txt`, for instance for a 10" 1024 × 600 screen:

```
video=HDMI-A-1:1024x600@60D
```

`D` forces the output on. Before Linux takes over the display, the screen may show its own "No
signal" message for a moment: that is normal.

## Screen upside down or sideways

Set **Screen rotation** in **Settings → [Audio & system](../../setup/settings/#audio--system)**,
or `display_rotation` to 90, 180 or 270 in [pedalboard.txt](../../setup/pedalboard-txt/).

## No sound

1. Check that the **IN** meter moves when you play. If it does not, the guitar is not reaching
   the pedalboard: check the cable and the interface's input gain.
2. In **Settings → Audio & system**, step **Interface** through the sound cards the system sees
   and pick yours. Over [SSH](../ssh/), `cat /proc/asound/cards` lists them too: `audio_device` in
   `pedalboard.txt` takes the name in brackets.
3. `tail -f /var/log/messages` shows why the audio server does not start.

Without an interface, the audio server waits for one: plugging it in later works.

## One output stays silent

With a multi-channel interface or sound HAT, an output can stay silent while its meter on the
[Routing](../../setup/routing/) page moves. The meter reads what OpenRig sends, so the sound is
lost after it. Over [SSH](../ssh/):

1. `grep -E "Interface|Connected|connect" /var/log/messages` shows the ports the interface offers
   and the connections OpenRig made at start: `pedalboard:out_3 → system:playback_3` means
   output 3 is wired. `jack_lsp -c` shows the live connections.
2. Many multi-channel cards and HATs have a volume or mute control per channel that starts at
   zero. `amixer -c <card> contents` lists them (the card number comes from
   `cat /proc/asound/cards`).
3. To test the card without OpenRig: `killall jackd; speaker-test -D hw:<card> -c 8 -t wav`
   plays a voice on each channel. The audio server starts again once the test ends (Ctrl+C).

## USB devices dropping out

A touchscreen or an audio interface that disconnects is usually short of power. The Pi 5 gives
USB devices 1.6 A only with the official **27 W** (5 V, 5 A) supply, and 600 mA otherwise.
Use that supply, or a powered USB hub.

## Clicks and crackles

See [Performance](../../setup/performance/#if-you-hear-clicks).

## A block went silent for a moment

If a plugin crashes while processing the sound, OpenRig catches it: the dry signal plays while
the chain is built again, so the sound comes back by itself. If it keeps failing, the screen
says so and the dry signal plays until your next change: remove the last block you added, or load
another preset. `grep -E "ERROR|WARN" /var/log/messages` shows what happened; send it with an
[issue](https://github.com/Siryu6/OpenRig/issues).

## The tone sounds wrong with a capture

Neural captures are trained at 48 kHz. Check that `sample_rate=48000` in `pedalboard.txt`.
Try **Normalize** on the block, and set **Input** so the capture is driven as intended.

## The mini PC does not start the image

The x86_64 image needs a CPU with AVX2 (Intel Haswell or newer, N100 included; AMD Excavator,
Zen). On a Celeron or Pentium without it, such as a J4125 or N4020, it stops right after the boot
loader. Check with `grep -o avx2 /proc/cpuinfo` from any Linux on that PC: see
[What you need](../../getting-started/hardware/#x86_64-mini-pc).

## A plugin does not show in Add effect

The plugin may need a host feature OpenRig does not provide yet, or be built for another
processor. The log says why it was skipped: `grep -i lv2 /var/log/messages`. See
[LV2 plugins](../../setup/lv2/).

## Still stuck

[Open an issue](https://github.com/Siryu6/OpenRig/issues) with the version (Settings → Updates),
your board and interface, and the output of `pedalboard-report` (see
[SSH and logs](../ssh/#logs-and-version)).
