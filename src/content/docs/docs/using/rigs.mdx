A **rig** is a whole [preset](../presets/): its blocks, splits and settings, its 8 scenes and its
tempo, packed so that another pedalboard can play it.

## On the rigs site

The OpenRig rigs site lists the rigs players publish. You can browse them, upvote them, keep
your favourites and comment on them.

## On the pedalboard

- **Install rig** fetches everything a rig needs: its plugins come from the
  [plugin store](../../setup/store/) and its amp, pedal and cabinet models from
  [TONE3000](../tone3000/), so they download on their own.
- A preset can be exported as an `.openrig` file, and an `.openrig` file imported, without
  going through the site.
