## Tuner

<Shot src={tuner} alt="The tuner showing A2 in tune, 110.2 Hz, +3 cents" caption="In tune: the note turns green, the LED ramp shows the cents." />

The tuner is chromatic and goes down to 38 Hz: drop tunings, 7 and 8 strings and bass guitars
work. It only listens while its page is open, so it costs nothing the rest of the time.

- **Mute while tuning** silences the outputs while the page is open, including the inputs played
  straight on an output (see [Inputs and outputs](../../setup/routing/)).
- **Reference** sets the pitch of A4, 440 Hz by default.

Open it from the **Tuner** tab, by holding the tap footswitch, or with MIDI CC68.

With a [rotary encoder](../../setup/footswitches/#the-encoder), a press on **Mute while tuning**
toggles it. On **Reference**, press, turn to change it by 1 Hz per click, then press to keep it
or hold to go back.

## Looper

<Shot src={looper} alt="The looper playing bar 2 of a 3 bar loop at 120 BPM, one layer, with Count-in and Quantize on" caption="The looper, playing a 3 bar loop with count-in and quantize on." />

The looper records what comes out of the effects, in stereo, up to 30 seconds. It plays on the
main mix only, not on the [sends](../../setup/routing/#send-and-return-blocks).

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Record** | Starts recording; pressing **Play** ends the loop and plays it |
| **Overdub** | Records a new layer over the loop |
| **Play** / **Stop** | Plays or stops the loop |
| **Undo** | Removes the last layer |
| **Clear** | Erases the loop |

The slider sets the loop's volume.

### Tempo, count-in and quantize

The bottom row sets the looper's tempo and bar:

- **BPM**: 40 to 240. It follows the [MIDI clock](../midi/) when one is received, and is locked
  then.
- **Bar**: 2 to 12 beats, shown as a time signature (3/4, 4/4…). 6, 9 and 12 beats are 6/8,
  9/8 and 12/8: the tempo then counts eighth notes.
- **Count-in**: **Record** first plays one bar of clicks, the downbeat higher, and recording
  starts on the next downbeat. The state shows **COUNT-IN** and the beat. **Record**, **Play**
  or **Stop** during the count-in cancels it.
- **Quantize**: ending the loop carries on to the end of the bar, so the loop is a whole
  number of bars. Ending it just after a bar line (within an eighth of a beat, 100 ms at most)
  ends it on that bar line instead. Overdubs keep the loop's length.
- **Click**: the volume of the click, by 10 %. It is heard on the main mix and never recorded.

These settings are kept with the session. The looper can also be driven over MIDI (CC60 to CC63, see
[MIDI](../midi/)).

With a [rotary encoder](../../setup/footswitches/#the-encoder), turn to go through the buttons
and press to trigger one. On the volume, press, turn to change it by 5 % per click, then press to
keep it or hold to go back.
