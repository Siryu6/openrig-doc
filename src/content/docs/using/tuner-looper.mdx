## Tuner

<Shot src={tuner} alt="The tuner showing A2 in tune, 110.2 Hz, +3 cents" caption="In tune: the note turns green, the LED ramp shows the cents." />

The tuner is chromatic and goes down to 38 Hz: drop tunings, 7 and 8 strings and bass guitars
work. It only listens while its page is open, so it costs nothing the rest of the time.

- **Mute while tuning** silences the outputs while the page is open, including the inputs played
  straight on an output (see [Inputs and outputs](../../setup/routing/)).
- **Reference** sets the pitch of A4, 440 Hz by default.

Open it from the **Tuner** tab, by holding the tap footswitch, or with MIDI CC68.

With a [rotary encoder](../../setup/footswitches/#the-encoder), a press on **Mute while tuning**
toggles it. On **Reference**, press, turn to change it by 1 Hz per click, then press to keep it
or hold to go back.

## Looper

<Shot src={looper} alt="The looper playing bar 3 of a 4 bar loop at 100 BPM, one layer: the bar number large, the beat LEDs, the time, and the waveform with a line on each bar and the playhead; below, the transport, Undo, Clear, Loop ×2, ½ Speed, Loops, the tempo with TAP, the bar, Count-in, Quantize, Click and its level, and the volume" caption="Playing bar 3 of a 4 bar loop: the waveform shows the bars and where the loop is." />

The looper records what comes out of the effects, in stereo, up to 30 seconds. It plays on the
main mix only, not on the [sends](../../setup/routing/#send-and-return-blocks).

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Record** | Starts recording; pressing **Play** ends the loop and plays it |
| **Overdub** | Records a new layer over the loop |
| **Play** / **Stop** | Plays or stops the loop |
| **Undo** | Removes the last layer |
| **Clear** | Erases the loop |
| **Loop ×2** | Doubles the loop: it plays twice, and later overdubs span the whole longer loop. Press again for ×4, as long as it fits in 30 seconds |
| **½ Speed** | Plays the loop at half speed, an octave down. Press again for full speed |
| **Loops** | Saves the loop, or loads one saved before: see [Saved loops](#saved-loops) |

The slider sets the loop's volume.

### What the screen shows

The top of the page shows the state (**RECORDING**, **PLAYING**, **COUNT-IN**…), the number of
bars and layers, and where the loop is:

- the **bar** being played, large, out of the bars of the loop (**3 / 4**), with a LED per beat
  and the time in small type. A loop that is not on the bar grid (recorded without quantize)
  shows the time large instead;
- the **waveform** of the loop, with a line on each bar and the playhead moving over it. It fills
  in as you record, and an overdub shows as you play it.

### Tempo, click, count-in and quantize

The bottom rows set the looper's tempo and bar:

- **BPM**: 40 to 240. **TAP** taps it (it counts when touched, not on release). It is the same
  tempo as the [Live page](../live/), the tap footswitch and MIDI CC64. It follows the
  [MIDI clock](../midi/) when one is received, and is locked then.
- **Bar**: 2 to 12 beats, shown as a time signature (3/4, 4/4…). 6, 9 and 12 beats are 6/8,
  9/8 and 12/8: the tempo then counts eighth notes.
- **Count-in**: **Record** first plays one bar of clicks, the downbeat higher, and recording
  starts on the next downbeat. **Overdub** pressed while the loop is stopped counts in too.
  The page shows **COUNT-IN** and the beat. Any transport button during the count-in cancels
  it, and **Undo** does nothing then. There is no count-in before **Play**: the loop is its own
  guide.
- **Quantize**: ending the loop carries on to the end of the bar, so the loop is a whole
  number of bars. Ending it just after a bar line (within an eighth of a beat, 100 ms at most)
  ends it on that bar line instead. Overdubs keep the loop's length.
- **Click**: a metronome while recording, playing and overdubbing, the first beat of each bar
  accented. With the click on, the loop is always a whole number of bars.
- **Click** *n* **%**: the volume of the click (and of the count-in), by 10 %. It is heard on
  the main mix and never recorded.

These settings are kept with the session.

### Half speed

**½ Speed** plays the loop at half speed, and so an octave lower, as on a Helix or a Ditto X4:
for a bass line under a riff, or to work out a fast lick. There is no speed change without a
change of pitch. An overdub always records at full speed: starting one brings the loop back to
full speed.

### Saved loops

<Shot src={loops} alt="The Loops list: Save loop at the top, then four saved loops with their bars, tempo, bar and length, each with Load and a bin" caption="The Loops list: save the loop, load or delete one." />

**Loops** opens the list of saved loops:

- **Save loop** saves the loop as it sounds, all layers mixed, under a name typed on the
  on-screen keyboard (the date and time when left empty). Finish an overdub before saving.
- **Load** replaces the loop with a saved one, stopped and ready to play, on its bars. A loop
  saved at another sample rate is converted. A file longer than the looper's 30 seconds is
  refused, with its length.
- The bin deletes one, after a confirmation. To rename a loop, save it again under the new name
  and delete the old one.

Loops are standard WAV files (32-bit float, stereo), with a small file next to each for its name,
bars, tempo and time signature. They are in the `loops` folder of the data directory:
`/data/pedalboard/loops` on the pedalboard (see [SSH and logs](../../help/ssh/#files)),
`~/.local/share/pedalboard/loops` with the desktop app on Linux. A WAV file copied there is
listed too, as a loop of free length.

### MIDI and encoder

The looper can be driven over MIDI: CC60 to CC63 for the transport and undo, CC66 for half speed
(0–63 full speed, 64–127 half speed). See [MIDI](../midi/).

With a [rotary encoder](../../setup/footswitches/#the-encoder), turn to go through Record,
Overdub, Play, Stop, Undo, Clear, Loop ×2, ½ Speed and Loops, then the tempo, TAP, the bar,
Count-in, Quantize, Click, the click level and the volume. Press to trigger a button. On a
value, press, turn to change it (the volume by 5 % per click), then press to keep it or hold to
go back.
