A **rig** is a [preset](../presets/) you can share: one `.openrig` file holding the chain
(blocks, settings, splits, its 8 scenes and its tempo) and the list of what it needs, the
plugins and the model files with where each one comes from. On another pedalboard, installing
the rig fetches what is missing and adds the chain as a new preset.

A rig never contains plugins or models, only references to them: plugins come from the
[plugin store](../../setup/store/), amp, pedal and cabinet models from [TONE3000](../tone3000/)
with your own account.

## On the pedalboard

On the **Presets** page, the share icon next to **Save** opens the **Rigs** panel. It has an
**Export** card at the top and the **Import** list under it.

### Export

The **Export** card exports the chain as it is now, under the name of the current preset.

- **Author**: your name, written in every rig you export. Set it once, it is kept. Leave it
  empty to stay anonymous.
- **MIDI CCs**: the MIDI controls learned by the blocks fit your own controller, so they are
  left out unless this is on.
- **Export** writes the rig to `rigs/<name>.openrig` in the pedalboard's data folder.
  **To USB** writes it to a USB drive too, when one is mounted.

The note under the title lists what only this pedalboard has: a plugin you copied by hand, or a
model or impulse response of your own. Those are listed in the rig by name only; it still
installs elsewhere, without them, but cannot be published on openrig.eu.

### Import

The **Import** list shows the `.openrig` files of the `rigs` folder and of the top folder of
mounted USB drives (marked **USB**). Tap one to see what it needs, item by item:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ON BOARD** | Already on this pedalboard |
| **STORE** | A plugin the store installs |
| **TONE3000** | A model downloaded with your TONE3000 account ([link it](../tone3000/#linking-your-account) first) |
| **MISSING** | Cannot be had here: the preset is installed without it |

**Install rig** installs and downloads what it can, and keeps going when one item fails. It
then saves the chain as a new preset, with the paths of this pedalboard, and loads it. Each item
then shows **INSTALLED** or **FAILED**, and why.

:::caution[USB drives on the OpenRig image]
The OpenRig OS image does not mount USB drives by itself yet: **To USB** and the USB part of
the list only work with the desktop app for now. On the pedalboard, copy rigs into
`/data/pedalboard/rigs` over the network instead (see [SSH and logs](../../help/ssh/#files)):

```bash
scp -O "Plexi Crunch.openrig" root@192.168.1.42:/data/pedalboard/rigs/
ssh root@192.168.1.42 'chown -R pedalboard:pedalboard /data/pedalboard'
```

and fetch the ones you export from the same folder.
:::

With the desktop app, the data folder is `~/.local/share/pedalboard` on Linux (or
`$PEDALBOARD_DATA_DIR`), and USB drives are found where the desktop mounts them
(`/media/<user>/` or `/run/media/<user>/` on Linux, `/Volumes/` on macOS). On Windows, copy
files into the `rigs` folder.

### Safety

Installing a rig never runs anything from the file. A rig is at most 1 MiB and is checked
before anything is installed; the files it names stay inside the models folder, and downloads
go where the app decides, not where the rig says. Plugins only come from the signed store
catalog, models only from TONE3000.

## On openrig.eu

[openrig.eu](https://openrig.eu/rigs) is where players share their rigs. Each rig page draws
the chain as the rack does, with its scenes and the list of what it needs.

- **Browse** the rigs: search, filter by style, tag or what they need, sort by trending, new,
  top or downloads. Anyone can download a rig, no account needed.
- **Sign in** with your email address to take part: the site emails you a one-time link, no
  password. A new address picks its handle.
  - **Upvote** the rigs you like and keep them in your **favourites**.
  - **Comment** on a rig, and get notified of replies.
  - **Publish** your own: upload an `.openrig` file exported from your pedalboard, with a
    description, tags and later versions. A rig with items only your pedalboard has (see
    [Export](#export)) cannot be published.
- **Link a pedalboard** to your account at [openrig.eu/link](https://openrig.eu/link) with the
  short code it shows (see below). Linked pedalboards are listed, and can be unlinked, in the
  site's settings.

## openrig.eu from the pedalboard

The **Rigs** panel has two tabs: **This pedalboard** (export and import, as above) and
**openrig.eu**. The pedalboard needs a network connection
([Wi-Fi](../../setup/settings/#wi-fi) or Ethernet); without one, the tab says so and the
pedalboard keeps playing.

### Browse and install

No account is needed to browse and install.

<Shot src={browse} alt="The openrig.eu tab of the Rigs panel: a search field, the Link button, the Trending, New, Top and Downloads sorts, style chips and three rigs with their STORE and TONE3000 badges, votes and downloads" caption="The openrig.eu tab: search, sorts, styles, and the rigs with what they need." />

- Tap the search field to search with the on-screen keyboard, pick a sort (**Trending**,
  **New**, **Top** of the week, **Downloads**) and a style. **Load more** shows the next rigs.
- Each card shows the rig's name, author, style, votes and downloads, with a **STORE** or
  **TONE3000** badge when it needs plugins from the store or models from TONE3000.
- Tap a rig to open it: its description, scenes, versions and address on the site, and on the
  right what it needs, each item marked when this pedalboard already has it.
<Shot src={detail} alt="A rig opened on the pedalboard: its description, scenes, version and address on the left, the Install button and the list of what it needs on the right, each item marked ON BOARD, STORE or TONE3000" caption="A rig: what it needs is on the right, Install at the top." />

- **Install** downloads the rig and shows the same plan as a file from the
  [Import](#import) list. It then installs what is missing and adds the new preset.

### Link your account

Linking lets the pedalboard use your favourites and publish in your name. It never sees your
email address or anything you type on the site.

1. On the **openrig.eu** tab, tap **Link**, then **Link this pedalboard**.
2. The pedalboard shows a QR code, a short code and `openrig.eu/link`. Scan the code with your
   phone, or open the address on any device, sign in and enter the code.
3. Approve the pedalboard on the site. A few seconds later the pedalboard says
   **Linked to openrig.eu as @you**, and the **Link** button becomes your account button
   (**✓ @you**).

<Shot src={link} alt="The Link this pedalboard dialog: a QR code, openrig.eu/link, the code CDD8-6M45, Waiting for approval and a Cancel button" caption="Linking: scan the QR code, or enter the code at openrig.eu/link." />

The code expires after a few minutes; **Cancel** stops waiting. To unlink, open the account
button (**✓ @you**) and tap **Unlink**: the pedalboard forgets its key and the site revokes it.
Unlinking it from the site's settings works too: the pedalboard notices on its next call.

### Favourites

Once linked, the tab shows **Browse** and **My favourites**. **My favourites** lists the rigs you added to your favourites, on the site or on the
pedalboard.
Tap one, then **Install**. On a rig, **Add to favourites** adds it; tap **Favourite** to
remove it.

### Publish a preset

Once linked, **Publish preset** shares the current preset:

1. The pedalboard exports it (without its MIDI CCs) and checks what it needs. Items only this
   pedalboard has, such as a plugin copied by hand or your own model or impulse response, cannot
   be had by others: they are listed, and publishing waits until you remove them or replace
   them with store plugins and TONE3000 models.
2. Pick a **style** and write a short **description** with the on-screen keyboard.
3. Tap **Publish**. The pedalboard shows the rig's address on openrig.eu, to share, and
   **Done** closes it.

<Shot src={publish} alt="The publish form: Publish Hard Rock as @Siryu, without your MIDI setup, the style buttons with Rock selected, a description and the Publish button" caption="Publishing a preset: a style and a few words." />

If you already published a rig with the same name, the form offers to publish a **new
version** of it instead (**New version of your rig "…"**): the style stays the one of the rig,
and **What changed** takes an optional changelog. Tap **Publish version**. Turn the switch off
to publish a separate rig.

<Shot src={newVersion} alt="The publish form for a new version: an empty description, the New version of your rig Hard Rock switch on, the changelog Less gain on the MT-2, chorus a bit wider, and the Publish version button" caption="A new version of a rig you already published, with what changed." />
