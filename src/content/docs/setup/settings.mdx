Open **⋮** → **Settings**. Changes apply at once; there is nothing to save.

The **⋮** menu at the top right holds, from top to bottom:

| Item | |
|---|---|
| **TONE3000** | The TONE3000 library, once your account is [linked](../../using/tone3000/#linking-your-account) |
| **Routing** | What each input and output of the interface does: [Inputs and outputs](../routing/) |
| **Updates** | The version installed, and the updates: [Updates](../updates/). A dot shows on **⋮** and next to **Updates** when an update is ready to install or waits for a restart |
| **Settings** | This page |
| **Power off** | Switches the pedalboard off, or restarts it. Outside the OpenRig OS, **Quit OpenRig** instead |

<Shot src={menu} alt="The ⋮ menu open over the rack: TONE3000, Routing, Updates with an orange dot, Settings and Power off; the ⋮ button has an orange dot too" caption="The ⋮ menu. The dot says an update is ready." />

The cards of Settings come in the order you need them: what you set up anywhere new first, what
you set once last.

| Card | What it sets | More |
|---|---|---|
| **Wi-Fi** | Scan, join, disconnect, forget a network; turn the radio on or off; the pedalboard's IP address | [Below](#wi-fi) |
| **Audio & system** | The audio interface, sample rate, buffer, screen rotation and Wi-Fi country of `pedalboard.txt` | [Below](#audio--system) |
| **Display** | Light theme | [Below](#light-theme) |
| **MIDI** | Devices found, channel, follow MIDI clock | [MIDI](../../using/midi/) |
| **Network MIDI** | MIDI from a computer over the network (RTP-MIDI): on / off, session name, devices allowed or blocked | [Network MIDI](../../using/midi/#network-midi) |
| **Remote control** | Your phone or tablet as the pedalboard's screen: on / off, password, hotspot | [Remote control](../remote/) |
| **TONE3000 Connect** | Link your TONE3000 account | [TONE3000](../../using/tone3000/#linking-your-account) |
| **Processing** | How many cores the effects may use: Auto, or 1 up to the number of cores | [Below](#processing) |
| **Hardware ›** | Opens the Hardware page: footswitches and their GPIO, tap tempo on the last one, the rotary encoder, knob encoders | [Footswitches and encoder](../footswitches/#settings--hardware) |

<Shot src={s2} alt="The whole Settings page: Wi-Fi, Audio & system, Display, MIDI, Network MIDI with a MacBook Pro connected, Remote control on, TONE3000 Connect signed in, Processing with DSP cores on Auto (4), and the Hardware entry" caption="The whole Settings page, from Wi-Fi to Hardware." />

The inputs and outputs and the updates used to be cards of Settings; since OpenRig 0.14.0 they
are in the **⋮** menu, as **Routing** and **Updates**.

With a [rotary encoder](../footswitches/#the-encoder), every control of Settings and of the
Hardware page can be reached without touching the screen: see [Settings with the
encoder](../footswitches/#settings-with-the-encoder).

## Light theme

The screen is dark by default, which reads best on a dark stage. Turn **Light theme** on in
**Display** for daylight: the pages get light panels and dark text, while the blocks of the rack,
the knobs, the faders and the footswitches keep their dark look. The choice is kept across
restarts.

<Shot src={light} alt="The rack with the light theme: light background and top bar, dark rack blocks" caption="The rack with the light theme." />

## Wi-Fi

Tap a network to join it, and type its password on the on-screen keyboard (**#+=** for
symbols). Joined networks are remembered on the data partition; **Forget** removes one. The
password is never shown or logged.

Ethernet needs no setting: plug the cable in.

The card also shows the pedalboard's **IP address** on each connected network, Wi-Fi and
Ethernet, for instance `192.168.1.42 (Wi-Fi)`: the address to use for [SSH](../../help/ssh/).

Set your country in **Wi-Fi country** of [Audio & system](#audio--system) to use the channels
allowed there.

## Audio & system

The settings that used to need a computer are on the screen too. **Audio & system** changes
[pedalboard.txt](../pedalboard-txt/), the file on the card's first partition, without taking the
card out:

<Shot src={top} alt="Audio & system: Interface Scarlett 2i2 USB with a ›, 48 kHz, 128 frames, 2 periods, screen rotation as 0° 90° 180° 270° segments, Wi-Fi country FR · France with a ›" caption="Audio & system: two lists, three steppers and the rotation segments." />

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Interface** | A list: Auto (the first USB interface or sound HAT), or one of the sound cards plugged in, by name |
| **Sample rate** | 44.1, 48, 88.2 or 96 kHz. Keep 48 kHz for neural captures |
| **Buffer** | 16 to 1024 frames per period |
| **Periods** | 2, 3 or 4 |
| **Screen rotation** | 0°, 90°, 180° or 270°, side by side |
| **Wi-Fi country** | A list: Default, or a country, for the channels allowed there |

**Interface** and **Wi-Fi country** show their value with a **›**: tap one to open the list of
choices, with the current one checked, and tap another. An interface chosen before and now
unplugged shows as *(not found)*. Sample rate, buffer and periods have **−** and **+**.

<Shot src={picker} alt="The Wi-Fi country list open over Audio & system: DE · Germany, DK · Denmark, ES · Spain, FI · Finland, FR · France checked, GB · United Kingdom" caption="The Wi-Fi country list, the current country checked." />

The card shows the latency of the buffer as you step through the values. Nothing changes until
you tap **Apply** (**Cancel** goes back): the audio settings restart the audio for a moment, a
new rotation turns the screen at once, and the Wi-Fi country applies at the next restart. The
file keeps your comments, and a wrong value can still be fixed from a computer.

The card shows on the OpenRig OS only; the desktop app has no `pedalboard.txt`.

## Processing

**DSP cores** sets how many processor cores the effects may use. On **Auto**, the default, a
heavy rig is spread over all of them: the four Cortex-A76 cores of a Pi 5, with no added latency
and the same sound as on one core. Light rigs stay on one core anyway. See
[Multi-core DSP](../performance/#multi-core-dsp).
