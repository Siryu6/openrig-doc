Use your phone or tablet as the pedalboard's screen: open its address in a web browser, sign in,
and play. Nothing to install. A pedalboard built without a touchscreen uses it as its only
screen; one with a touchscreen gets a second one, in your hand.

The browser has two modes:

- **Editor**: the pedalboard's own screens (rack, presets, store, settings…), on your tablet or
  phone turned sideways. Tap, drag, scroll and type as on the touchscreen.
- **Live**: a portrait page for the stage: presets and scenes, setlist slots, tap tempo, the
  tuner, the meters, and every block with its bypass and its controls.

The remote control is off until you choose a password.

## Turn it on

### With a touchscreen

**Settings** → **Remote control**: turn it on and choose a password (8 to 63 characters). The
card shows the addresses to open, how many devices are connected, and the hotspot. **Change
password** and **Sign out all devices** are there too.

### Without a screen

Before starting the pedalboard, put the card in a computer and edit
[`pedalboard.txt`](../pedalboard-txt/):

```ini
remote_password=choose-8-to-63-characters

# Optional: a Wi-Fi network to join
wifi_ssid=Home
wifi_password=its-key
```

At the next start, the pedalboard keeps them and replaces them in the file with `(saved)`: the
card holds no password afterwards. Write a new value to change one, or `remote_password=off` to
turn the remote control off.

Then, on your phone or tablet, either:

- join the pedalboard's Wi-Fi, **OpenRig-XXXX** (its key is the remote control password), and
  open **http://10.42.0.1**;
- or, on your own network, open `http://` and the pedalboard's address (your router lists it
  as `pedalboard`).

## Without a touchscreen

With a remote control password and no touchscreen (no display, or a monitor without touch),
the pedalboard's screens are for your phone or tablet, and a connected monitor keeps the
OpenRig logo. `display` in `pedalboard.txt` chooses by hand:

| `display` | |
|---|---|
| `auto` (default) | The touchscreen if there is one, else your phone or tablet (with a password set) |
| `screen` | Always the touchscreen |
| `remote` | Always your phone or tablet |

## The pedalboard's hotspot

When there is no network to share, the pedalboard brings its own: **OpenRig-XXXX**, protected by
the remote control password. `remote_hotspot` in `pedalboard.txt`, or the buttons in
**Settings** → **Remote control**:

| `remote_hotspot` | |
|---|---|
| `auto` (default) | Started when the pedalboard has no network 45 s after starting |
| `on` | Always (the pedalboard does not join another Wi-Fi then) |
| `off` | Never |

The hotspot reaches only the pedalboard, not the Internet. Some mini PCs have Wi-Fi cards that
cannot make a hotspot: the pedalboard says so, and the remote control still works over your
network.

## Safety

- Only devices on your local network can connect, and only with the password. Wrong passwords
  slow down after three tries.
- **Sign out all devices** (or a new password) disconnects everyone at once; so does a restart.
- On someone else's Wi-Fi (a venue, a hotel), other people on that network can see what the
  pedalboard and your phone exchange. Use the pedalboard's hotspot, or a network you trust.
- Anyone with the password controls the whole pedalboard. Sharing the hotspot shares the control.
