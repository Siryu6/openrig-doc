A backup is one file with your presets, setlists, rigs, loops and settings. Use it before you
write a new card, to move to another OpenRig, or to go back after a mistake.

Open **⋮** → **Settings** → **Backup** (the last card).

:::note[After OpenRig 0.14.3]
Backup comes with the first release after OpenRig 0.14.3.
:::

## Back up

Plug in a USB drive (FAT32, exFAT or ext4) and tap **Back up to USB drive**. The file,
`openrig-backup-<date>-<time>.tar.gz`, goes at the top of the drive. Then tap **Eject**, under
the button, before you pull the drive out.

With no USB drive, the button reads **Back up on the pedal**: the backup stays on the pedal
(the last 3 are kept). Download it from the [remote control](#from-the-remote-control).

**Include models and plugins** adds your amp and pedal models, impulse responses and LV2
plugins. The file can then be large: the sizes show under the switch. Without them:

- plugins from the [store](../store/) and models from [TONE3000](../../using/tone3000/) are
  listed in the backup, and the pedal downloads them again after a restore;
- models of your own and plugins you copied by hand cannot be downloaded again. The page warns
  you when you have some: turn the switch on to keep them.

:::note[Never in a backup]
Passwords and accounts (the remote control password, your TONE3000 and openrig.eu sign-ins) and
Wi-Fi networks are never saved. A restore leaves them as they are. On a new pedal, sign in again.
:::

## Restore

The Restore list shows the backups found on USB drives (**USB**) and on the pedal (**PEDAL**).
**FULL** marks a backup with models and plugins. Tap one: OpenRig shows what it replaces and what
it keeps, part by part.

<Shot src={confirm} alt="The restore dialog: Settings, Presets, Setlists, Rigs, Loops, Models and Plugins, each replaced, with the number of files in the backup and now, and the Restore and restart button" caption="What the restore replaces, before anything changes." />

**Restore and restart** restarts OpenRig, which swaps the data before it loads anything. After
the restart, a message says when the backup was made and what is left to download.

- **Download** (on the Backup page) installs the store plugins and TONE3000 models the restored
  presets need. It needs a network, and a linked TONE3000 account for the models.
- **Undo restore** brings back the data you had before the last restore. Undo again to go back
  to the restore.

OpenRig checks the whole file before it changes anything: a damaged file, or one with
something unexpected inside, is refused and nothing changes. A power cut during a restore is
safe: the next start finishes it.

Plugins are built for one kind of board. Restored on another (a Raspberry Pi backup on a mini
PC), they are left out, and the dialog says so: download them again from the store.

## From the remote control

On the [remote control](../remote/), the **Status** tab has a **Backup** section:

- **Download backup** saves a backup on your phone or computer. Tick **Include models and
  plugins** for the large files.
- **Restore** sends a backup file to the pedal. The pedal checks it, then restarts to restore it.

A file sent or received this way is 512 MB at most. For larger backups, use a USB drive.

## On a computer

The desktop app has the same page. USB drives are found where the desktop mounts them
(`/media/<user>/` or `/run/media/<user>/` on Linux, `/Volumes/` on macOS). After a restore, the
app starts again by itself.
